SOPA! Is Now Open in Cedar Crest

Savory and sweet stuffed sopapillas, counter service and to-go, at 12220 NM Hwy. 14.

For immediate release · November 9, 2026 · Media contact: Christian Manzer, hello@fivem.group

CEDAR CREST, N.M. — SOPA! Seriously Stuffed Sopapillas is now open at 12220 NM Hwy. 14 in Cedar Crest, following a soft opening on October 25. The counter-service, to-go concept serves savory and sweet stuffed sopapillas, with two stuffed SOPAs per order.

The savory side includes New Mexican classics like green chile chicken, a pulled pork recipe first created for another Primera Agua concept, and a chicken salad SOPA. A berry dessert SOPA leads the sweet side, along with a few more adventurous combinations. Sides and canned drinks round out the menu.

“SOPA! is a very, very simple stuffed sopapilla concept,” said Christian Manzer, co-owner of FiveM Group. Manzer co-owns SOPA! with Jesse and Holly Manzer, who also operate Primera Agua Hospitality.

All three owners are East Mountain natives who commute into Albuquerque for work, and SOPA! is a way to bring their recipes home. “We want to do what we can to support the job market up here,” Manzer said. The location opens with about 10 team members and is designed to grow, with plans to expand.
